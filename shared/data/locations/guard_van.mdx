Last car of the consist, and the only one with a door that opens onto nothing
but weather.

## Appearance

Crates roped to the wall, a stove nobody has lit, and the guard's ledger open on
a nail. The lamp swings wider here than anywhere forward, which is how you know
you have reached the end of the train.

## What Is Kept Here

Everything nobody wanted in their berth. The ledger records what was loaded at
Paris and what was loaded at Calais, and the two hands do not match.
